More experiments

Shorter projects, described without code.

Behaviour cloning

Behaviour cloning for a farm-management agent

Kaggle simulation competition (30 in-game days, 720 turns, two farms sharing one market). Cloned the top players from 8.9 M state–action pairs in their replays. The first clone hit 98.8% action accuracy and still passed every turn: replaying the games showed the replay format stores the action that produced each observation, one turn late. Fixed, plus an opening book and a cleaner corpus, the agent went from 2,422 to 96,332 coins and won 6 of 12 games against top-10 agents. A Bradley–Terry ranking rebuilt the official top 10 (8 of 9) without seeing it.

Not submitted in the end; kept as a study in verifying data by replaying it

About

I came to data science from law and economics. I studied both at ITAM, then a Master's in Human Rights (ITAM – University of Genoa, double degree) and a PhD at Universidad Panamericana, currently in the final stage. Today I am doing the Master's in Data Science, Big Data & Business Analytics at Universidad Complutense de Madrid.

I like problems where the data has to be built before it can be modelled: turning sports video into tracking data, a video game into a physics simulator, or thousands of court rulings into something a lawyer can search. I measure everything against a reference, and I write down what did not work.
