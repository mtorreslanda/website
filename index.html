How it is made

Each robot is drawn on a 16 × 17 grid, one cell at a time, with no images. A seeded random generator (mulberry32) picks one option for each of ten slots, from antenna to colour, which gives 368,640 possible robots. The same robot is this tab's icon and the small one on the page, which treats lines of text as platforms and follows your cursor.

Other experiments
01Behaviour cloning for a farm-management agentBehaviour cloning · PPOKaggle simulation competition (30 in-game days, 720 turns, two farms sharing one market). Cloned the top players from 8.9 M state–action pairs in their replays. The first clone hit 98.8% action accuracy and still passed every turn: replaying the games showed the replay format stores the action that produced each observation, one turn late. Fixed, plus an opening book and a cleaner corpus, the agent went from 2,422 to 96,332 coins and won 6 of 12 games against top-10 agents. A Bradley–Terry ranking rebuilt the official top 10 (8 of 9) without seeing it.Not submitted in the end; kept as a study in verifying data by replaying it
