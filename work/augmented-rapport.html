Augmented Rapport

A local tool for online therapists: it describes the non-verbal side of a video session (speaking time, prosody, facial and head movement, synchrony) and never diagnoses.

The idea

Therapists who work online already record sessions. Augmented Rapport turns the recording into a session report: a timeline with speaking turns, silences, prosody and movement, a measure of non-verbal synchrony between the two people, and a draft note written by a local language model. The interpretation stays with the clinician.

How it works

  1. Sessiontwo video + audio streams
  2. SpeechWhisper transcript, turns, prosody
  3. Face & headMediaPipe landmarks → movement
  4. Synchronyvs shuffled pseudo-synchrony
  5. Reporttimeline + draft note, local LLM

Validation, honestly reported

Every indicator ships with its own validation and a control, and the larger tests were pre-registered before looking at the results.

  • Works: smile detection (AUC 0.99), speaker attribution (F1 0.85), reliability of the indicators (14 of 14 with ρ ≥ 0.67), and non-verbal synchrony that is clearly above shuffled pseudo-synchrony in ~300 long conversations.
  • Does not (yet): synchrony does not predict the rapport people report after the conversation beyond trivial baselines. It is reported as a negative result, not hidden.

Principles

Describe, do not infer emotions. Process locally, never in the cloud. Use only components with commercial licences.

About the data. Validation used licensed research corpora that cannot be redistributed; no participant data is shown. The demo clip comes from a publicly available mock session, credited to its source.
